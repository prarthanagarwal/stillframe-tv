# Product
## Register
product
## Users
The owner of a MiTV-AXSO1 and their household, using a D-pad remote from a sofa. Preserve voice search, Chromecast, installed streaming apps and HDMI.
## Product Purpose
A personal Home screen with a slim glass dock, a landscape wallpaper, weather, time and date. Settings on the TV must support selecting dock apps, arranging them, choosing a wallpaper and changing weather location. Holding OK on an app exposes dock actions. No recommendation preview or content feed.
## References and personality
The slim translucent dock in matheus-caldeira/cleanyourtv-ui, and the working native CleanYourTV navigation. Quiet, personal, readable.
## Anti-references
Sponsored rows, recommendation feeds, shopping and TV manufacturer promotions.
## Design principles
Keep the landscape visible. Put choices in Home Settings. Keep remote navigation deterministic. Preserve system functionality and provide recovery.
## Accessibility
Large remote targets, visible focus, native text controls and existing TalkBack navigation; honor reduced motion.
